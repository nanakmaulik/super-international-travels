GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Helpful information before you start your visa enquiry.

Do Indian citizens need a visa for Singapore?+

Indian passport holders generally require a visa for social or business visits to Singapore. Requirements depend on nationality and immigration status.

How long does visa processing usually take?+

The Singapore High Commission in New Delhi generally advises 3–5 working days after submission, excluding the submission day, weekends and public holidays. Some cases take longer.

Is approval guaranteed?+

No. The Singapore authorities alone decide whether to issue a visa. A visa does not guarantee admission at the border.

Are you an authorised Singapore visa agent?+

Super International Travels is presented as an independent travel consultancy. This website does not claim direct authorisation by the Singapore High Commission. A visa application must use a valid authorised submission channel.

Is ₹3,634 the government visa fee?+

No. The official Singapore visa processing fee is ₹2,100 on the current New Delhi High Commission page. The illustrative higher total includes additional service components. Final fees must be confirmed before payment.

IMPORTANT

Singapore visa decisions, validity and permitted length of stay are determined by Singapore authorities. Travellers remain responsible for entry requirements, including the SG Arrival Card where applicable. Independent consultancy support is not a visa approval or government service.
